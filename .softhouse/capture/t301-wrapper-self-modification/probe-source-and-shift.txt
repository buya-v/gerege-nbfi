=== PART 2 — INSERTING BYTES ABOVE THE UNREAD TAIL (the T288 shape) ===
Same inode every time. The question is not 'does the tail change' — T309
settled that — it is 'does a SHIFTED offset resume on a line boundary'.

=== PART 3 — HOW FAR AHEAD HAS ZSH ALREADY READ? ===

window is smaller than it looks. Walk the tail's distance from the sleep and
find the boundary where an in-place rewrite stops reaching the running shell.
