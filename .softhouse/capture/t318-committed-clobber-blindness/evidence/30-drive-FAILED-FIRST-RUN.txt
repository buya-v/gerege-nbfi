T318 instrument 30 — FIRST RUN, ALL 15 ARMS FAILED. Committed deliberately.

Cause: `local name="$1" d="$ROOT/$name"` in mk(). Bash expands EVERY argument
to the `local` builtin before assigning any of them, so `$name` was still
unset when `$ROOT/$name` was expanded, and `set -u` aborted the function.
Every arm therefore reported "could not build scratch clone" and the drive
printed PASS=0 FAIL=15.

This is recorded because the drive's whole subject is instruments that report
a reassuring answer without having looked. An instrument that had counted
"could not build scratch clone" as anything other than FAIL would have been
the same defect one level up. It counted them as FAIL, which is why this
transcript exists rather than a green one.
