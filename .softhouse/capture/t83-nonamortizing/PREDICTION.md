## The mechanism I am predicting from

Named by T74 and confirmed at every cited line by T75, in pinned Fineract
`426a23544e8426a38ae43ae404670a0a7e85b9eb`:

1. `RepaymentPeriod.isFullyPaid()` (`:371-372`) is
   `getEmiPlusCreditedAmountsPlusFutureUnrecognizedInterest().isEqualTo(getTotalPaidAmount())` —
   `0 == 0` when the period's EMI quantizes to zero, so it is **vacuously true**.
2. With every period "fully paid", `findLastUnpaidRepaymentPeriod` is empty and
   `calculateLastUnpaidRepaymentPeriodEMI` (`:1160`) takes its `:1178-1181` fallback.
3. That fallback's last filter (`:1180`) is `rp.getOutstandingLoanBalance().isGreaterThanZero()`,
   which **populates** the target period's balance `Memo` while its `duePrincipal` is still 0.
4. `:1210` then raises that period's EMI through a plain `@Setter`. `emi` is **absent** from the
   memo's dependency array (`RepaymentPeriod.java:400`), so the balance is never recomputed and the
   emitted column is stale.

**Therefore the predicted trigger is exactly "the level EMI quantizes to 0.00".** Nothing else in
the chain depends on the principal or the term.

## The closed form I predict, stated in advance

With `B` the principal in **minor units**, `r = annualRate / 100 / 12` (the DAYS_30/DAYS_360 monthly
rate) and the ordinary annuity factor `a(r, n) = r / (1 − (1+r)^−n)`, the level EMI in minor units is
`B · a(r, n)`. Quantized to scale 2 under HALF_UP it is zero iff

```
B · a(r, n) < 0.5
```

so I predict

```
largest failing principal  = ceil(0.5 / a(r, n)) − 1   minor units
smallest clean principal   = that + 1
```

and, because `a` is strictly decreasing in `n`, I predict the failing region **grows with the term**
and is **empty at n = 2** for every rate I test (`0.5 / a < 1`, so no principal of at least one minor
unit fails).

I also predict the region is **contiguous**: every `B` from 1 up to the boundary fails and every `B`
above it is clean, with no holes. If the sweep finds a hole, the closed form is refuted as a
*characterisation* even if it is right about the endpoints.
