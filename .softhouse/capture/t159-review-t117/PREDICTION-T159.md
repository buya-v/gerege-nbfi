## Money discipline (P-25)

`gencases_t159.py` constructs no float. Every principal is an **integer count of minor units**
reaching Java as `new BigDecimal(<int>).movePointLeft(2)`; the rate is the decimal **string**
`"600.0"` handed verbatim to `new BigDecimal`. The classifier `census_t159.py` (added with the
observation) parses every money value by splitting the oracle's `BigDecimal.toPlainString()` output
on `.` and padding to the currency's decimal places, and decides every residual by **integer
subtraction**.
