THE ORACLE ACCEPTS defineOpeningBalance ON AN EMPTY LEDGER, AND WRITES TWO JOURNAL ENTRIES PER LEG. OB-ACCEPT-01 sent POST /journalentries?command=defineOpeningBalance with three legs -- DEBIT 250000.25 on GL 2, DEBIT 100000.37 on GL 3, CREDIT 350000.62 on GL 4 -- to a tenant whose findNonContraTransactionIds is EMPTY, and the oracle returned HTTP 200 and SIX entries on one transaction: each leg, and a CONTRA entry of the opposite side on the financial-activity-300 account (GL 1). The transaction id itself is server-assigned, is carried in request.transaction_id for the record, and is NOT a graded cell -- a re-run of the recipe produces a different one and every graded cell unchanged. A port that refuses every opening balance -- the reasonable-looking thing to do, since the rule at :717 reads a repository query and not the request body -- is what this vector exists to catch, and until this capture existed nothing in this corpus could.
