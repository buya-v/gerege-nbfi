THE ORACLE ACCEPTS A MANUAL JOURNAL ENTRY DATED ONE DAY AFTER THE LATEST GLCLOSURE, AND WRITES THE CALLER'S THREE LEGS AND NOTHING ELSE. T327's B-1 arm created a GLClosure at office 1 with closingDate 2026-08-26, proved on the SAME INSTANCE MINUTES EARLIER that the closure was live and its boundary INCLUSIVE -- an entry dated ON 2026-08-26 drew HTTP 403 error.msg.glJournalEntry.invalid.accounting.closed, and one dated 2026-08-25 drew the same code echoing the CLOSING date -- and then POSTed a balanced three-leg body dated 2026-08-27, closingDate + 1 and still on or before the business date. The oracle returned HTTP 200 and wrote exactly three journal entries on one transaction: DEBIT 250000.25 on T327-1000, DEBIT 100000.37 on T327-1100, CREDIT 350000.62 on T327-2000, no contra expansion. THE ONLY DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE REFUSAL AND THE ACCEPTANCE IS ONE DAY. Until this vector the store pinned only the REFUSING side of :636, so a port that refused EVERY entry carrying a closing date passed LDG-REFUSE-04 and survived the entire corpus -- the identical mutant shape T305 killed for opening balances (T296 arm A).
