THIS VECTOR IS GRADED BY REPLAY AGAINST A PORT. THE RELATION IS WHAT IT ASSERTS, NOT THE CALENDAR. Given a tenant whose business date is B, with NO GLClosure for the entry's office, and a balanced manual journal entry dated T with T == B: THE ORACLE ACCEPTS IT WITH HTTP 200 AND WRITES ONE JOURNAL ENTRY PER REQUEST LEG, each carrying entry_date == T. Equality is the whole point -- the guard at :630 is isAfter and is STRICT, so T == B is NOT in the future. Here T = B = 2026-08-28, a calendar date in Asia/Ulaanbaatar (+08), the tenant zone. THE COMPANION HALF OF THE INSTRUMENT, which a re-run must also reproduce: the SAME shape dated B + 1 is REFUSED with HTTP 403 error.msg.glJournalEntry.invalid.future.date. Accept at B and refuse at B+1 is what fixes B, and it is what makes this vector a measurement rather than a transcription.

RE-DERIVATION RUNS THE RECIPE, NOT A RE-READ OF A TENANT. `bash .softhouse/capture/t327-closure-accepting-side/throwaway/run-all.sh` rebuilds a throwaway instance from the same image, replays the setup campaign, and fires this body dated (today in Asia/Ulaanbaatar) against a tenant with no closure. It must reproduce every graded cell: HTTP 200, and THREE journal entries on one transaction in this order -- DEBIT 250000.250000 on T327-1000, DEBIT 100000.370000 on T327-1100, CREDIT 350000.620000 on T327-2000, i.e. 25000025 / 10000037 / 35000062 minor units, totals 35000062 and 35000062. WHAT IT CANNOT REPRODUCE BYTE-FOR-BYTE, DELIBERATELY: the transaction id (server-assigned, not a graded cell), the captured-at timestamp, and THE DATES -- they are computed relative to the day it runs, because "on the business date" is by definition today. A re-run reproduces the RELATION with different literals. That is T289's date strategy (c).

A WARNING ABOUT THE HOUR, WHICH IS PART OF THE RECIPE AND NOT AN ANECDOTE. This capture was taken at 17:33Z, an hour in which UTC and Asia/Ulaanbaatar disagreed about the date. A re-run whose HOST computes "today" in UTC while the tenant computes it in Asia/Ulaanbaatar will, between 16:00Z and 24:00Z, fire an entry dated B - 1 and observe an acceptance that proves nothing about the boundary. The rig computes the date in the TENANT zone; a re-derivation that changes that has changed the experiment. NEVER HARD-CODE AN OFFSET to "fix" this -- read the tenant's zone.

THE INSTANCE IT WAS TAKEN ON NO LONGER EXISTS, BY DESIGN, and THIS VECTOR MUST NEVER BE RE-FIRED AT THE STANDING REFERENCE ORACLE: its body is a valid, balanced, postable manual journal entry, and an accepted journal entry cannot be deleted. The standing oracle's counters were identical before, during and after the run that produced these bytes.

IF A FUTURE FINERACT REFUSES AN ENTRY DATED EXACTLY ON THE BUSINESS DATE -- i.e. if :630's comparison ever becomes non-strict -- THIS VECTOR IS FALSE AND MUST BE RETIRED, NOT EXEMPTED (P-8). It is the only observation in this store that would notice.
