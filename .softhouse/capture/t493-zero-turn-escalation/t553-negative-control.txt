The FIRST implementation of this fix normalised digits away per line, and
atk-legit went RED at "1 NOVEL substantive added surface line(s) of 12" — twelve
rows of a numeric table differ only in their numbers. That false RED is why the
per-line normalisation preserves digits. It was found by driving the control, not
by reasoning about it.
