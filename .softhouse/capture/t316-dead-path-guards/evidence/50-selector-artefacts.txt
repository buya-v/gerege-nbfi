HAD THIS SHIPPED, it would have accused `check-capture-namespace.sh` -- T299's guard, landed two
commits before this task -- of naming a dead path, when the path it names is the OWNER record
T299 created and which is present. It would have accused `conformance.sh` references of being
dead. It would have put 56 rows of slander against nine other tasks' committed evidence into a
pinned file that every future fire reads.

THE FIX, in `census_dead_paths.py`: a literal RESOLVES if it exists verbatim OR after trailing
sentence punctuation is stripped. The stripped form is consulted ONLY to say YES, so it can never
turn a live reference into a dead one -- the fix cannot hide a real defect. An ellipsis (`...`
or `…`) is now INDETERMINATE, because a truncation mark is the author saying they elided the
rest of the path.
