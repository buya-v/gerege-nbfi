INTERPRETATION

- No trial hung (hung_past_30s=False on all 9). Every SIGTERM was answered.
- The relevant number for DRIVER_STOP_GRACE_SECS is cluster B, not cluster A:
  a real fire's `claude` spends effectively its whole lifetime "mid-task"
  (hours of agentic work), so a stop signal overwhelmingly lands there, not
  in the ~0.2-0.5s startup window cluster A modelled.
- Cluster B: max 2.591s, mean ~1.16s, n=6.
- fire-program.sh sets DRIVER_STOP_GRACE_SECS=5 (was 3, chosen by T211
  against an instantly-dying /bin/sleep stand-in) -- roughly 2x the observed
  max, not merely clearing it, because of the gap this calibration does NOT
  close: it used a single-turn, tool-free `-p` call. A real fire's `claude`
  runs `/softhouse-program` with Bash/Edit tool calls and possibly MCP
  connections, none of which this calibration exercised, and any of which
  could plausibly add its own shutdown latency (child processes, open
  sockets) on top of what a bare text completion shows. That gap is recorded
  as UNVERIFIED in the T217 handoff, not papered over with a number this
  calibration didn't actually produce.
