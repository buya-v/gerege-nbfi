--- WHY.  `[^#\n]` is not the same bracket expression in the two engines.
    Python re : \n inside a bracket expression is the NEWLINE escape, so
                [^#\n] = 'any char except # and newline'.

                [^#\n] = 'any char except #, backslash and the LETTER n'.
    The anchored [^#\n]* therefore cannot cross a letter `n`, and every
    occurrence of `trap` in the corpus has an `n` earlier on its line
