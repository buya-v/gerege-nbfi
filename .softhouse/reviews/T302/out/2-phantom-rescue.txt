--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The stale `index.lock` is not a contrived fault. It is what a `git add` interrupted by
SIGKILL leaves behind, and a SIGKILLed worker is precisely the population this sweep
exists to serve. Any other cause with the same signature (read-only FS, disk full,
corrupt ref, a `WB` that already exists) produces the identical outcome, because the
outcome does not depend on WHY git failed — none of the three rcs is read.

Contrast fire-program.sh:783-797, the MAIN-TREE rescue in the same function, which reads
ADD_RC and COMMIT_RC and logs "NOTHING was rescued" on either failure. T202's own comment
there says why, in the past tense, about this exact failure mode: "the guard still logged
`rescued: committed the leftovers`". The fix was applied to one branch of the function and
not to the other.
