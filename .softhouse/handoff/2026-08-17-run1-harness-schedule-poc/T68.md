## 6. The corrected mechanism (brief item 4) — re-derived from the Java, and it is RIGHT

I re-opened every cited line rather than reading T64's quotation of it.

* `EmiAdjustment.shouldBeAdjusted()` [VERIFIED: `EmiAdjustment.java:31-36`, read in full] is
  `floor(n/2) > 0 && !emiDifference.isZero() && emiDifference.abs().multipliedBy(100)
  .isGreaterThan(originalEmi.copy(lowerHalfOfRelatedPeriods))`.
* `Money.copy(double)` is `copy(BigDecimal.valueOf(amount))` [VERIFIED: `Money.java:220-222`] →
  `new Money(this.currency, amount, this.mc)` [VERIFIED: `Money.java:216-218`] → the constructor at
  `Money.java:40-53` sets `this.amount = amountScaled.setScale(...)`. **The amount is REPLACED, not
  scaled.** `originalEmi` contributes only its currency and `MathContext`.
* `multipliedBy(100)` binds the **`long`** overload [VERIFIED: `Money.java:380-390`; `int → long` is
  more specific than `int → double` under JLS most-specific-method resolution].
* Therefore, in minor units at 2 decimal places: `|emiDiff|_minor > floor(n/2)`. For `ZP-A`:
  `28 > 28` → **false**, loop never runs. For `ZP-B` (n=55): `28 > 27` → **true**, loop runs.
  The port's `shouldBeAdjusted` computes `left = |diff| · 100` against `threshold = floor(n/2) · 10^2`,
  i.e. `2800 > 2800` → false — a faithful integer translation [VERIFIED: `emi.go:933-942`].

**And §2.4's mechanism is false, which I confirmed two independent ways:**

1. *By algebra.* Had `shouldBeAdjusted` returned true on `ZP-A`, the `:1271` break would **not** have
   fired: `adjustment() = emiDifference.dividedBy(56)` = `0.28/56 = 0.005` major, and
   `Money.of(…)` re-quantizes at 2 dp **HALF_UP** → `0.01`, so `adjustedEmi = 0.02 ≠ originalEmi 0.01`.
   [VERIFIED: `Money.java:352-358` `dividedBy(long)`, `Money.java:40-53` constructor]
2. *By measurement.* If `:1271` broke the loop regardless, flipping the guard alone would change
   nothing. It changes 126 cells by up to 28 minor units. Refuted.

## Should any promoted vector be withdrawn?

**No.** Withdrawing any of the four would cost, concretely:

* `T64-ZP-B` — the **only** vector in the corpus that grades `applyFinalPeriodResidual`'s self-re-entry
  (`ProgressiveEMICalculator.java:1211-1214`). Withdraw it and T59's "no vector grades that shape"
  blocker is reinstated, and the O(n²) function becomes unfixable-with-confidence again.
* `T64-ZP-A / C / D` — the only vectors that place `|emiDifference|` exactly on `floor(n/2)`, which is
  the only way to grade the **strictness** of `EmiAdjustment.shouldBeAdjusted`'s comparison. Withdraw
  them and `>` versus `>=` on that one line becomes invisible again — a defect that moves 28, 17 and 36
  minor units and rewrites the whole schedule.
* All four — the only rows in the corpus with `principal_minor == "0"` and non-zero interest, and the
  only rows that are entirely dead after an early payoff.

The dust-region objection is real and T64 states it first and loudest, but DEC-1 answers it in terms:
*"There is NO principal size below which this is safe."* A corpus that refuses to look at the rounding
floor is not more conservative — it is blind at the one place the arithmetic is decided.
