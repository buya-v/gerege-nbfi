## The closed form, falsified

T83 labelled `B_minor × a(r,n) < 0.5` a **HYPOTHESIS CONSISTENT WITH** the measurement rather than a
fact. **That was the right call and T84 vindicated it**; `gates.md` now says so in those words.

I evaluated the closed form in **exact rational arithmetic** over all 342 non-calibration cells of
T84's two captures: **320 held, 22 refuted, 0 exact ties**. **Every refutation is a family-B cell**
(600.0 % / B = 1 / n ≥ 104), where the exact gap `B·a − ½` is *positive* — `+2.429e-19` at n = 104
falling to `+3.025e-36` at n = 200 — so the closed form predicts CLEAN and the oracle fails.

Over **T83's own 330 cells** the same exact-rational evaluation holds **330 of 330** — so the closed
form is an excellent description of family A on the grid it was fitted to, and it is family B that
breaks it.

**Count correction, recorded in `gates.md`:** T84 reports **18** refutations; I get **22** over the
same cells. The four extra are T84's own probe-1 tie cells `T84-TIE-R600p0-N{108,120,150,200}-B1`,
which its `prediction.json` registers as `predictedFails: false` and which measured FAIL. Same
conclusion either way; 22 is the number I can show.

That the sub-ulp gap explains the failure (the EMI quantizing to zero at 19 significant digits) is
an **explanation, not a verified mechanism** — marked `[UNVERIFIED]` in `gates.md`.
