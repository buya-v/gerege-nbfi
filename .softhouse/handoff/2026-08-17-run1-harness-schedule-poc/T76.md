## Vectors promoted or NOT, and why

**PROMOTED: NOTHING.** Full analysis: `.softhouse/capture/pathb/t76/PROMOTION-DECISION.md`.

The binding rule in the brief has two conjuncts — a field the frozen contract returns, **and** a
`capabilities.json` entry reading `exercised`. The second conjunct passes for all four
(`path_b_server` marks `installment.rounding.multiple` and `daysinyear.custom.strategy`
`exercised`). **The first fails for all four**, and there is a third blocker the brief did not name:
the contract's *pinned oracle inputs*.

* **B-02** — the one that would grade something real. `installment.rounding.multiple.in_graded_domain`
  is `false`, and the contract says why in its own words: *"InstallmentRoundingMultipleMinor stays in
  this contract and is refused for Run 1 (DEC-1 section 4.7)"* [VERIFIED: `contract.go:114-118`],
  with the graded-domain predicate `InstallmentRoundingMultipleMinor == 0` [VERIFIED:
  `contract.go:1130`]. A parity vector carrying `100` would assert numbers where the ratified
  contract requires `ErrNoDiscriminatingVector`. **DEC-1 is ratified → amendment is a gate.**
* **B-03 / B-04** — worse than ungraded: the frozen `GenerateRequest` has **no component at all**
  for the input, and **pins it to null** as one of six oracle-input constants [VERIFIED:
  `contract.go:1183`, reasoned at `:1246-1264`, which itself sequences the question: *"it becomes a
  contract question the moment DayCountActualActual enters the graded domain, and not before"*].
  Promotion needs a **shape change to the frozen contract** — a hard `user` gate — plus ACT/ACT in
  the graded domain first.
* **B-01** — sound and admissible, and it **kills nothing new**. Its money is identical, cell for
  cell in integer minor units, to the already-promoted Path A vector `P-MNT-1M2` (12/12 periods and
  the totals; `t76/out/crosscheck.txt`). Separately it is not truthfully expressible: its product is
  ACT/ACT [VERIFIED: `req/product-1-baseline.json` `daysInYearType: 1`, `daysInMonthType: 1`], so a
  faithful transcription writes `day_count: "ACTUAL_ACTUAL"` and lands outside the graded domain
  (`REFUSE-01` already carries that refusal); writing `FIXED_30_360` to slip past the predicate
  would state a product setting the oracle did not have.

**Promoting any of them would be a rejection, and promoting none is the correct outcome here.**

### Gate raised — proposed **G-7** (three ordered questions, one decision)

1. *(prerequisite, already open as **G-4**)* does `DayCountActualActual` enter the graded domain?
2. **DEC-1 §4.7** — lift the Run-1 refusal of `InstallmentRoundingMultipleMinor` and flip

I did **not** amend DEC-1, `contract.go`, `capabilities.json`, `PIN.json` or any vector.

## Money-math notes

Everything below was computed in **exact integer minor units** (`json.loads(..., parse_float=Decimal,
parse_int=Decimal)`, exact ×100 scaling with an integrality assertion). No float, no tolerance, no
rounding of a comparison, anywhere in my scripts.

**1. `installmentAmountInMultiplesOf = 100` (B-01 → B-02), measured today.** 12 of 12 periods move.
The level installment goes **11,208,237 → 11,210,000** minor units (+1,763 on each of periods 1-11);
the final installment goes **11,208,240 → 11,186,622** (−21,618); total repayment −2,225. The final
period is deliberately **not** the rounded EMI — it absorbs the whole signed residual
(`ProgressiveEMICalculator.java:1202-1205`), and here the residual is negative. Note also, from T22
and unchanged by me: the rule is **round to the NEAREST multiple under the tenant rounding mode**,
not "raise to the next multiple", with a zero-guard.

**2. `daysInYearCustomStrategy` (B-03 → B-04).** 12 of 12 periods move; total interest
**14,465,921 → 14,501,143**, i.e. **+35,222 minor units** (T22's "delta 352.22" in major units,
confirmed independently).

**3. B-01 ≡ `P-MNT-1M2`.** All 12 periods and all totals equal in minor units — two seams (in-JVM
embeddable and full REST stack), two day-count configurations, one arithmetic.

**4. A real defect in the existing property invariants, found by mutation and closed.** T22's ten
invariants *are* failable — one minor unit on `principalDue` trips I1/I5/S2, one on
`totalDueForPeriod` trips I4/I5 (transcripts in `t76/out/mutation/`). But **a one-minor-unit change
to `totalOriginalDueForPeriod` trips nothing**: the `*Original*` and `*Outstanding*` mirror columns
are never read by any of the ten. A port emitting a wrong ORIGINAL column would have passed every
property check in this program. `t76/t76_mirror_invariant.py` (**I7**) asserts the mirrors equal the
DUE columns on an unpaid schedule; PASS on all four captures plus the canary, FAIL on the mutant.
This bears directly on B-02: the ORIGINAL column is where a rounded installment must also land.

**5. Invariants on the T76 captures:** the ten T22 invariants **ALL PASS** on all four
(`t76/out/invariants.txt`) — including *principal amortizes to zero* (I2), *splits sum to whole*
(I5), *sum(principal) = disbursed* (I1) and *every money literal integral in minor units* (S1) — and
I7 passes on all four plus the canary.

**6. B-03/B-04 from source.** T30's from-source model (DAILY / ACT-ACT cross-year partial-period
arm, `ProgressiveEMICalculator.java:1400-1414`, `calculatePeriodFractions:1550-1568`), which carries
zero hard-coded expected values and uses exact `Decimal`, was **executed by me this fire** against
the production-settings re-captures: **CONSISTENT for B-03 and B-04 at both HALF_UP and HALF_EVEN
model modes** (`t76/out/rederive-check-t36paths.txt`). I did not write a fourth independent model;
I verified the existing one runs and agrees, and I read enough of it (`grep` for hard-coded
observed values → **0 hits**) to satisfy myself it is a derivation and not a transcription.
