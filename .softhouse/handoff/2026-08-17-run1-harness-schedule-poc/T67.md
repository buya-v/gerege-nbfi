# (2) THE CARRIED INSTALLMENT COUNTER — equivalence, reasoned then observed

## The proof, done before I looked at T65's evidence

Old: `InstallmentNumber: installmentNumberOf(periods)` where `periods` is the rows emitted **so far**, and
`installmentNumberOf` returns `1 + |{rows of kind DownPayment or Repayment}|`.
New: `nextInstallment := int32(1)`, read into the row, then `nextInstallment++` immediately after each
repayment row is appended.

**Invariant, by induction on the emission loop:** at every point, `nextInstallment == 1 + (payable rows
appended so far)`. Base: 0 payable rows, counter 1. Step: the only two `append`s into `periods` in the
whole function are the disbursement row (`generator.go:456-468`, not payable, does not increment — and the
old helper's `Kind` predicate skipped it identically) and the repayment row (`:470-478`, payable,
increments exactly once). Therefore the read at `:472` equals `installmentNumberOf(periods)` at that same
point. **Value-identical at every row, by construction, not by measurement.**

## The adversarial cases the brief asked me to construct — none of them exists

| case | verdict |
|---|---|
| disbursement row at index **0** | covered: `pending && inPeriodM3(...)` fires in iteration 0; counter untouched; old helper counted 0 payable rows and returned 1. **Identical.** |
| disbursement row at an interior index | identical for the same reason; the skip is the *absence* of an increment on one side and the *absence* of a `Kind` match on the other. |
| disbursement row at the **last** index | **impossible.** The disbursement row is appended at the top of an iteration and that iteration unconditionally appends its repayment row at the bottom; the only `break` (`generator.go:452-454`) is before the disbursement emission. Even were it possible, nothing reads the counter afterwards. |
| **consecutive** disbursement rows | **impossible.** `generate` reads `req.Disbursements[0]` only and latches `pending = false`; `validateGradedDomain` never admits a second one. |
