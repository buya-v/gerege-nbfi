## ITEM 1 — the settlement

### The port gap, restated

`ProgressiveEMICalculator.java:1217` calls
`calculateUnrecognizedInterestTillDateOnScheduleModelCopyAndDefer`, which at `:1243-1251`
uses `getPeriodWithUnrecognizedInterest` (`:1805-1814`) to find a period whose

That much is not in dispute and T63 stated it correctly.

### Why nobody could settle it before

`futureUnrecognizedInterest` **cannot be read off a returned schedule.** It feeds
`getDueInterest()`, and `getDueInterest()` is
`min(calculatedDueInterest, emiPlusCreditedAmountsPlusFutureUnrecognizedInterest)`
**[VERIFIED: `RepaymentPeriod.java:271-286`, `:293-295`]**. On a zero-EMI period that
minimum is `min(cdi, 0) = 0` **whatever `cdi` is**. Every capture pass in this program
recorded only the returned plan, so the corpus was structurally blind to the field. This is
a fresh instance of **P-14** — *a mutation no vector can distinguish is a blind spot, not an
absence* — one layer up: **a FIELD no capture records is a blind spot, not a zero.**

### The proof — from the pinned source

Setting: the Path A generate seam inside DEC-1's graded domain
(`conformance/admit.go:999-1060`) — exactly one disbursement, `RepaymentEvery` 1 `MONTHS`,
`DECLINING_BALANCE`, `FIXED_30_360`, no down payment, no installment rounding multiple, MNT
2 decimals, `(19, HALF_UP)` — plus the seam's two hard-wired nulls, `loanCharges` and
`holidayDetailDTO` **[VERIFIED: `ProgressiveLoanScheduleGenerator.java:83`]**. Nothing is
ever paid.

Write `S_k` for period *k*'s interest-period sum, `emi_k` for its installment, `cdi_k` for
`calculateCalculatedDueInterest()`, `u_k` for `getUnrecognizedInterest()`.

**(a) The lookup runs on a copy whose later rate factors are ZEROED.** The method
deep-copies at `:1226` and calls `calculateRateFactorForScheduleTillDateInclusive(copy,
tillDate)` at `:1237`, which sets `rateFactor` and `rateFactorTillPeriodDueDate` to
`BigDecimal.ZERO` on every interest period with `targetDate.isBefore(ip.getDueDate())`
**[VERIFIED: `:1791-1803`, filter at `:1799`, zeroing at `:1800-1801`]**.
`InterestPeriod.getCalculatedDueInterest` multiplies by `getRateFactorTillPeriodDueDate()`
**[VERIFIED: `InterestPeriod.java:145-157`]**, so a zeroed interest period contributes
exactly `0`.

**(b) `tillDate` is anchored at the DISBURSEMENT, not at maturity.** The ordinary path
reaches `calculateLastUnpaidRepaymentPeriodEMI(scheduleModel, calculateFromRepaymentPeriodDueDate)`
at `:747`, and that argument is `getEffectiveRepaymentDueDate(...)` — the due date of the
period the disbursement lands in, or the next period's due date when it lands exactly on a
due date **[VERIFIED: `:730-752` with the call at `:747`; `:146-151` -> `:250-263`]**. Call
that period's index `f`; on the ordinary shape `f = 0`. Hence **`S_k = 0` on the copy for
every `k > f`.**

**(c) Beyond `f` the chain is a pure non-increasing cascade.** With payments, credits,
capitalized income, fixed interest and `futureUnrecognizedInterest` all zero,
`calculateCalculatedDueInterest` reduces to `cdi_k = S_k + u_{k-1}`
**[VERIFIED: `RepaymentPeriod.java:252-265`, the previous-period term at `:262`]** and
`getDueInterest` reduces to `min(cdi_k, emi_k)` **[VERIFIED: `:271-286`]**, so
`u_k = max(0, cdi_k − emi_k)` **[VERIFIED: `:381-383`]** and for `k > f`
`u_k = max(0, u_{k−1} − emi_k)`, which telescopes to
`u_k = max(0, u_f − Σ_{j=f+1..k} emi_j)`.

**(d) An aggregate identity has just been enforced.** Immediately before the lookup,
`:1189-1207` computes
`diff = totalDisbursed + capitalizedIncome + creditedPrincipal + totalDueInterest − totalEMI`
and assigns `emi_L := emi_L + diff` on the selected last-unpaid period `L`
**[VERIFIED: `:1187-1207`]**. So `Σ_j emi_j = P + I`, with `P` the disbursed principal and
`I = Σ_j dueInterest_j` as measured on the real model just before the assignment.

**(e) Therefore `u_L = 0`, always.** `emi_j = 0` for every `j > L`, because `isFullyPaid()`
is `emiPlusCreditedAmountsPlusFutureUnrecognizedInterest == totalPaidAmount`
**[VERIFIED: `RepaymentPeriod.java:371-373`]** and with nothing paid that is `emi_j == 0`.
Also `emi_j = 0` for every `j < f`: those periods lie outside the related window and
`calculateEMIOnActualModel` writes the level installment onto window periods only
**[VERIFIED: `:735-745` with the window from `getRelatedRepaymentPeriods`,
`ProgressiveLoanInterestScheduleModel.java:191-198`]** — observed at `T66-M-DISB-ON-DUE`,
whose period 0 reports `emi == "0.00"`. Hence

    Σ_{j=f+1..L} emi_j  =  (P + I) − Σ_{j≤f} emi_j  =  (P + I) − emi_f,

and `u_L = max(0, u_f − (P + I) + emi_f)` with `u_f = max(0, cdi_f − emi_f)`.
If `cdi_f ≤ emi_f` then `u_f = 0` and `u_L = 0` immediately (since `emi_f ≤ P + I`).
Otherwise `u_L = max(0, cdi_f − P − I)`, and `dueInterest_f = emi_f` gives `I ≥ emi_f ≥ 0`,
so **`u_L = 0` as soon as `cdi_f ≤ P`** — which holds whenever the first period's rate
factor is at most `1.00` (100% per period), because `u_{f−1} = 0` (periods before `f` have
zero balance and zero installment) makes `cdi_f = S_f = B·r ≤ P·r`. At `FIXED_30_360`
monthly the per-period factor is `annualRate / 12`, so the sufficient condition is
`annualRate ≤ 1200%` on the regular lattice, `≤ 600%` where the month-end re-anchor
doubles a `periodRatio`. Above that the inequality is not tight and the conclusion is
carried by the search and the capture rather than by the closed-form bound — which is why
`T66-M-R12000` and `T66-M-DRIFT-R12000` (rate factor **10.00**) are in the capture.

**(f) And the cascade is dead from `L` on.** `u_L = 0 ⇒ cdi_{L+1} = 0 ⇒ u_{L+1} = 0 ⇒ …`.
`getPeriodWithUnrecognizedInterest` needs `u > 0` **strictly after `L`**
**[VERIFIED: `:1806-1808`]**. There is none. ∎

**(g) The `:1178-1181` fallback cannot rescue it.** It fires only when the first filter is
**empty**, and then selects the **last** period, after which nothing is strictly later
**[VERIFIED: `:1176-1181`]**.

**This closes exactly the gap T69 named.** `emi.go:315-325` says the argument *"still needs
the tillDate period's OWN unrecognized interest to be zero on every graded shape … that

## ITEM 2 — the smoothing loop's trial copy

**Verdict: no admissible input separates the two allocation sites. Inert today.**

The oracle allocates once **[VERIFIED: `:1274-1276`, `if (newScheduleModel == null) {
newScheduleModel = scheduleModel.deepCopy(mc); }`, outside any per-iteration reset]**; the
port allocates per iteration **[VERIFIED: `emi.go:1156`, `trial := m.deepCopy()` inside the
loop]**. On pass ≥ 2 they can only differ in a field pass 1 wrote to the trial that pass 2's
stamp does not overwrite.

1. **The stamp overwrites every window period.** `:1279-1286` writes `emi` and `originalEmi`
   on each period in the related window; its two extra conditions are
   `!adjustedValue.isLessThan(period.getTotalPaidAmount())` — always true with nothing paid —
   and `!period.isReAgedEarlyRepaymentHolder()` — false on generate. Then
   `calculateOutstandingBalance` recomputes every balance from the segments and
   `calculateLastUnpaidRepaymentPeriodEMI` re-derives the residual. Rate factors are never
   recomputed inside the loop and are identical in both allocations.
2. **So only a write OUTSIDE the window can survive**, and there are exactly three writers
   in `calculateLastUnpaidRepaymentPeriodEMI`:
   - **the guard loop `:1163-1174`.** Its predicate is
     `rp.getOutstandingPrincipal().isGreaterThan(totalDuePaidDiff)`.
     `getOutstandingPrincipal()` is `negativeToZero(getDuePrincipal() − getPaidPrincipal())`
     **[VERIFIED: `RepaymentPeriod.java:462-464`]**, which with nothing paid is
     `getDuePrincipal()` = `max(negativeToZero(emiPlusCredited+FUI − dueInterest), paidPrincipal)`
     **[VERIFIED: `:345-350`]**. A period outside the window has `emi == 0`, `credited == 0`
     and — by ITEM 1 — `FUI == 0`, so its due principal is `0`. And `totalDuePaidDiff` is
     `getTotalDuePrincipal() − getTotalPaidPrincipal()` = Σ credited amounts = the disbursed
     principal `P > 0` **[VERIFIED: `ProgressiveLoanInterestScheduleModel.java:347-348, 365-367`]**.
     `0 > P` is false. **The guard cannot write outside the window.** (This is T63's argument;
     I re-derived it and it holds.)
   - **the residual `:1207`, on the last-unpaid period `L`.** `L ≥ f` always, because every
     period before `f` has `emi == 0` and nothing paid and is therefore `isFullyPaid()`; and
     the window is exactly indices `f..n−1` **[VERIFIED: `getRelatedRepaymentPeriods`,
     `ProgressiveLoanInterestScheduleModel.java:191-198`]**. So `L` is **inside** the window
     and the next pass's stamp overwrites it.
   - **`futureUnrecognizedInterest` at `:1250` and `interestMovedUpward` at `:1249`.** By
     ITEM 1 they are never written on this domain. **This channel is real and is worth
     naming**: `:1183` resets `futureUnrecognizedInterest` on the **target period only**,
     while `:1185` resets `interestMovedUpward` on **all** — so a stale FUI on a non-target
     period WOULD persist across passes. It carries nothing here only because ITEM 1 holds.
3. **And on the ordinary graded shape there is no "outside the window" at all**: `f = 0`, so
   the related window is every period. The only admitted shape with `f = 1` is the
   disbursement-on-a-repayment-due-date family (`T66-M-DISB-ON-DUE`, promoted vector `P-03`),
   and step 2 covers it — observed at `T66-M-DISB-ON-DUE`, whose period 0 has
   `emi == "0.00"`, `duePrincipal == "0.00"` and `balance == "0.00"`.

**This is a stronger statement than T63's**, which rested only on the guard-loop predicate.
It adds: the residual write is always inside the window; the stamp's own skip conditions are
unreachable; and the FUI/IMU channel is only closed because ITEM 1 closes it.

### The precondition under which it starts to diverge — record this, do not rediscover it

Any **one** of the following makes `trial := m.deepCopy()` inside the loop a real divergence
from the oracle, and the port must then hoist the allocation out of the loop:

- **multi-tranche disbursement** — a later tranche puts `f > 0`, so periods exist outside
  the window on an ordinary schedule;
- **any payment** — `totalPaidAmount != 0` makes the stamp's
  `!adjustedValue.isLessThan(period.getTotalPaidAmount())` guard **skip** a window period,
  which then keeps pass 1's value; and it breaks `isFullyPaid() ⟺ emi == 0`;
- **credited principal/interest or capitalized income** on a pre-window period — makes its
  due principal positive so the guard loop at `:1163-1174` can fire there;
- **re-aging** — `isReAgedEarlyRepaymentHolder()` true makes the stamp skip a window period;
- **`futureUnrecognizedInterest` becoming reachable** (see ITEM 1's lapse list) — `:1183`
  resets it on the target only.

**Not fixed. Reported.** The port's comment at `emi.go:1122-1127` describes the rebuild
correctly and is silent on the allocation site, which is the part that differs.

## Vectors promoted (or why none)

**None, deliberately.**

Promotion discipline requires that *some named counterfactual moves one of the vector's
cells*. The pass-3h mechanism columns are **not cells the frozen contract returns** —
`contract.Period` carries kind, installment number, from/due dates, principal, interest and
outstanding balance, and nothing else. A port that omitted `futureUnrecognizedInterest`
entirely (which is what this port does) produces byte-identical contract output on every one
of these 18 shapes, so a vector transcribed from them would grade **nothing**. Promoting one
would inflate the corpus count without adding a single graded cell — the exact failure P-1
and T55-N2 warn about, in its opposite key.

Two of the eighteen cases (`P-CAL-ZPA`, `P-CAL-ZPB`) are re-observations of shapes that are
**already** promoted parity vectors; this run reproduces them cell for cell, which is a free
cross-pass control on `T64-ZP-A` and `T64-ZP-B` and is recorded as such. The other ten
mechanism probes are **eligible** for promotion as ordinary parity vectors on their money
columns — several reach rates and terms no promoted vector reaches — but transcribing and
counterfactual-measuring ten new vectors is a `test_writer` task of its own size and is
recorded as follow-up **F-3**, not smuggled into this one.

## Money-math notes

- **Every number in this handoff is either an integer count or a decimal string lifted
  verbatim from the capture.** `check-prediction.py` and `dump-rows.py` compare strings and
  never parse a money value into a float; `Capture3h.java` emits every `BigDecimal` through
  `toPlainString()`, as every pass since 3b has.
- The port-side search reads the port's own `int64` minor-unit quantities
  (`interestChainUpTo`, `emiMinor`) and does integer arithmetic only. No float appears
  anywhere in the throwaway harness.
- The one place a magnitude matters to the proof is step (e)'s sufficient condition, a
  **rate-factor** bound (`r ≤ 1`), not a money bound; it is stated as an exact rational
  condition on `annualRate / 12` and not as a decimal.
- `MoneyHelper.PRECISION` is a compile-time 19 and the tenant mode is HALF_UP (ordinal 4);
  the capture asserts the effective `MathContext` is `(19, HALF_UP)` from inside the JVM
  and the runner refuses the run otherwise.
