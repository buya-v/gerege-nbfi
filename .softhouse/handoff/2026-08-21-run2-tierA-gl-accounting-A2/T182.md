### Is `TieredStopAtLevel=1` a control that discriminates JIT-state, or does it change other things?

It changes **more than one thing**: compilation tier, therefore inlining depth, therefore compiled
frame size, therefore frames-per-fixed-stack — and speed (flat ~6.1 s per attempt vs 11–15 s for a
default-JIT throw; C1-only reaches overflow *faster*, which is what a *larger* frame predicts). So it
does **not** isolate the mechanism.

What it **does** discriminate, and this is its real value: it leaves interpreter profiling, code-cache
warming, data-cache warming and GC state intact while removing C2, and the transition disappears.
That rules out "generic repetition warms something" as *sufficient*. The honest statement is
**"the transition requires C2"**, not "C2's frame-size reduction is the mechanism" — and T177 says
exactly that, marking the mechanism `[UNVERIFIED]`. Its section title (d) *"The optimising compiler
(C2) is the warming agent"* edges past that, but the body pulls it back.

### Is attempt 5 a stable boundary, or one warm-up path?

7/7 JVMs, zero variance, and I made it 8/8. But all are the same cell, image, host and ambient load,
and T177 rightly refuses to call 5 a constant. **What T177 left on the table is that its own
committed data brackets the threshold with no new oracle time:** the two conditions that flip the
cell sit at ≈10,000 and ≈12,000 prior inner-period iterations (50 × n=200, and 4 × n=3000); the two
that do not sit at 200 and 2,000 (1 × n=200, 10 × n=200). The threshold therefore lies between
2,000 and 10,000 — **bracketing the measured `Tier4InvocationThreshold = 5000`**. That is genuinely
*establishing* evidence for the C2 attribution. `[INFERENCE by T182 — assumes the hot method runs
~once per repayment period; I did not instrument the counters.]`

## `errorStackDepthTotal`: cap or depth — my derivation

1. The field is `Throwable.getStackTrace().length` at the catch site
   [`.softhouse/capture/lib/ThrewOutcome.java:121,128`] — read by me, not inferred.
2. HotSpot truncates the backtrace it records at throw time to `MaxJavaStackTraceDepth`.
3. That flag's value **in the pinned image** is `1024 {product} {default}`, read out of the image's
   own `-XX:+PrintFlagsFinal` dump `[VERIFIED: out/jvm-defaults.txt]` — not from documentation.
4. **The argument T177 did not make, and the decisive one:** across every `-Xss` series — 512k, 1m,
   2m, 4m and the 2040k default — the recorded value is **exactly 1024 with zero variance** over
   ~72 throws. An **8× change in thread stack size cannot leave the true overflow depth
   bit-identical.** Invariance under a variable that must move it proves it is a cap.
5. With `-XX:MaxJavaStackTraceDepth=0` the same rig records 4683 / 5119 / 8400.

**(1)–(5) settle it: 1024 is the cap, and this is a measurement, not an inference.** T177's follow-up
5 is correct and belongs in `patterns.md`.

**Not settled:** the uncapped series' *shape* (n = 1 JVM, 4 points, non-monotone — the micro-fix),
and whether true depth at 512k differs from 4m at all, which was never measured, only capped away.
