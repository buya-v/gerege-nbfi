### The defect, named

T224 built a sweep, never ran it against a **known positive**, and then reported *"no fourth or
fifth surviving live assertion of the claim exists in this repository"*. The sweep's measured
recall on its one known positive was **0/9**. A search whose recall is zero on the instance you
already hold cannot license a statement about the instances you do not.

Three separable mechanisms, all reusable across this program:

1. **Anchoring an inflected stem.** `\bexist\b` excludes `existing`/`exists`/`existence`. English
   states absence with a gerund at least as often as with a finite verb. Anchor the LEFT of a stem
   if you must; never the right.
2. **Enumerating spellings instead of the concept, then calling the enumeration exhaustive.** T224
   *did* widen its terms (it added five) — widening a list of surface strings does not convert it
   into a search for a claim, and T224's handoff presents the widening as if it had.
3. **Triage at file granularity.** T224 states it did not open every hit of the ~150-file generic
   population. `main.go` was in it, via a line about something else. The per-file question ("is
   this file about the claim?") answered *no*; the per-line question at `:1`, never asked, answers
   *yes*.

### Candidate pattern — **P-72 (proposed)**

> **A sweep is an instrument. Calibrate it on a known positive before you report its negatives.**
>
> Before a repository sweep may support the sentence "the population is closed", it must be run
> against **at least one instance of the target that is already known to exist** — normally the very
> hit the task was dispatched to fix — and that run must be shown to HIT. A sweep that cannot find
> the defect in your hand has an unknown, possibly zero, recall, and its silence about the rest of
> the tree is not evidence. Corollaries: **commit the commands, not a prose description of them**
> (T224's terms are unreproducible from its handoff); **never right-anchor an inflected stem**
> (`\bexist\b` misses `existing` — the single character that hid this defect for a whole fire);
> and **triage per LINE, not per FILE** (`main.go` was inside T224's population and was dismissed
> whole).
>
> Evidence: T227 measured T224's stated terms at **0/9 recall on the hit T224 itself fixed**, and
> **0/9 on the hit it missed** (`ledgerguard/main.go:1`), while a single unanchored `exist` hits
> both. This sits directly under **P-66/P-70** ("not found" is a statement about the search) and
> supplies the missing *procedure*: P-70 says state where you looked; P-72 says prove the
> instrument fires before you quote where you looked.
