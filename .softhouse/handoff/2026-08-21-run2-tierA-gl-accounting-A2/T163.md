### The mechanism, and why `parse_float=Decimal` alone is **not** the fix

The brief offered `parse_float=Decimal` **or** integer minor units. I took a third option and here is
the reasoning, because A2-11's own follow-up FU-1 proposed a variant that would have introduced a
different corruption:

- `parse_float=Decimal` fixes the **read**. It does not fix the **write**: `json.dumps` cannot
  serialise a `Decimal`.
- A2-11's FU-1 suggested `default=str`. **That renders a Decimal as a JSON *string*** — `1200000.00`
  becomes `"1200000.00"`. That is a **wire type change**, a different corruption of the same body.
  I did not take it. `[VERIFIED: A2-11.md:522-525]`
- **So `resolve8.py` does not re-serialise the template at all.** It parses only to *validate* and
  *locate*, then splices the placeholder span into the template's own bytes. Every literal that is
  not the placeholder is preserved **by construction**, because it is never converted to any numeric
  type on the way out. Numbers are read through hooks that keep the **original source text**, so the
  value spliced in is the oracle's own digits.
- `parse_constant` **refuses** `NaN`/`Infinity`/`-Infinity` — Python-json extensions that are binary
  floats in every parser that accepts them, and the one remaining float ingress. Neither script had
  ever been tested on one.
- `float` is constructed **nowhere** in `resolve8.py`.
