## Adjudication — A2-17's "decide P-6/P-7 BEFORE P-1…P-5"

**SPLIT IT. A2-17 is right about P-6 and overreaches on P-7.**

- **P-6 (`capabilities.json`) — YES, before P-1…P-5.** The registry is shared **by design** (§5.2
  lists it among the shared parts); **DEC-2 itself declines to decide it** (§4.10's closing paragraph:
  "not DEC-2's to make"); and decisively, **§5.4's sequencing rule cannot be obeyed without it** —
  step 2 says author rows `false` *first*, and a builder cannot author even a `false` row without
  knowing which file it lives in. That is a real ordering inversion. F1 strengthens this: the
  store-level files are the only natural chokepoint at which a mislabelled context could be refused.
- **P-7 (`PIN.json`) — NO, not as stated.** Its premise — *"a second context implies a second pinned
  contract file"* — **is contradicted by §1.1**: DEC-2 creates and freezes **no** Go file and carries
  no PIN digest. There is nothing to pin. The real, narrower question is what `dec1_revision` a
  ledger vector declares (`admit.go:122` checks it per-vector against the single pin) — and that is
  answerable only **after** P-1 fixes the vector's shape.

```
P-6  ->  P-1…P-5  ->  P-7 (re-scoped to the dec1_revision question)
P-8  ->  independent, dispatchable NOW, in parallel
```

**Recommend:** adopt A2-17's ordering **for P-6 only**, and re-scope P-7 in §5.3 as part of repairing
F1.
