### WHERE I PUT (b), AND WHY NOT WHERE T359 MEASURED IT

T359 measured a one-line change at `impl.go:276-279` re-returning the residue refusal as a

1. **422 is a fabricated observation.** No oracle, no port and no wire ever produced it. This

   status into the *port's* answer so that a comparator will accept it is the exact shape the

So the routing decision is made in `gradeOne`, **where the class is known**, and applies to the
divergence class alone. `impl.go` is unchanged on that path; every other class still gets

## 2. THE CLASS, AND WHY IT CANNOT HOLD A FLOAT

`ClassDivergence` = `"divergence"`; expectation shape `expect.kind: "port-refusal"`.

**The hard constraint was to record `100.125000` MNT — a value no `int64` count of minor units
can hold — without a float anywhere near a money path.** Four things do that, and three of them
are enforced rather than promised:

1. **THE MONEY CELLS ARE NOT EMPTY, THEY ARE ABSENT.** `admit.go`'s `divergenceReasons`
   refuses `expect.legs`, `expect.total_debits_minor`, `expect.total_credits_minor`,
   `expect.refusal` and `expect.http_status` on a divergence vector. So no author is ever put
   in the position T352 was put in — having to write `10012` or `10013`, numbers neither system
   produced, to file the observation at all. It is not that the number is hard to choose; the
   field is not there. Driven red by
   `TestDivergenceMoneyCellsAreForbiddenNotMerelyEmpty` (four arms, one per field).

2. **THE OBSERVATION IS CHARACTERS, AND THE TYPE HAS NOWHERE ELSE TO PUT IT.**
   `OracleAcceptance` carries `HTTPStatus int`, `ObservedAmountTexts []string`,
   `WhyUnrepresentable string`, `Gate string`. No `float64`, no `json.Number`, no widened
   decimal, no `big.Rat`, no numeric named anything like an amount. `PortRefusal` carries two
   strings and nothing else.

3. **THE CHARACTERS ARE BYTE-CHECKED, NOT TRUSTED.** Every entry of `observed_amount_texts`
   must occur **verbatim** — `bytes.Contains`, nothing else — in the bytes of the artefact
   `provenance.capture_ref` names, and every `request.legs[].amount_major_text` must occur
   verbatim in `provenance.request_capture_ref`. Not "equal after parsing", not "equal after
   normalising": any of those would put the amount through a numeric type on the way to being
   checked, and the value in question is one no numeric type here can hold. Driven red both
   sides by `TestObservedCharactersMustBeInTheCitedCapture`.

   This is also what caught a real transcription slip in the banked candidate: it put the
   **readback** text `100.125000` into `request.legs[].amount_major_text`, where the caller
   actually sent `100.125`. Two renderings of one observation; the vector now carries each on
   its own side, anchored to its own capture.

4. **"IS IT ACTUALLY UNREPRESENTABLE" IS DECIDED ON BYTES.** `hasResidueBeyondMinorUnit` finds
   the `.` by index, walks the fraction one byte at a time and compares each byte with `'0'`.
   No `strconv`, no arithmetic, no division, no exponent. **The amount is never a number in
   this program, at any point, including intermediate calculation.**
   `TestTheResidueScanNeverBecomesANumber` tables values chosen so a float64 implementation
   would answer differently — 19 significant digits, a residue in the 17th, `0.000000000000000001`.

   It is also the **vacuity guard for the class itself**: a divergence vector whose observed
   texts are all representable is INADMISSIBLE, because that would be a way to move an ordinary
   parity vector out of the parity tally and keep the run green.
   `TestARepresentableAmountIsNotADivergence`.

Two further default-denies: `oracle_accepted.gate` and `oracle_accepted.why_unrepresentable`
must be non-empty (a recorded divergence nobody owns is worse than none), and
`oracle_accepted` is refused on **any other class**, so the field cannot accumulate silently on
the vectors that predate it.

### What the class GRADES: two structural cells, zero money cells

`divergence.port_outcome` (REFUSED / ACCEPTED) and `divergence.port_refusal_marker`. The
first is the divergence itself; the second stops the first being satisfied by *any* refusal —
a port that refuses for an unrelated reason would otherwise be counted as agreeing. The marker
must be ≥ `MinPortRefusalMarker` (12) characters and must occur in the recorded full text
(`TestThePortRefusalMarkerCannotBeVacuous`, three arms).

**Zero money cells is a property, not a gap**, and it is why
`EXEMPTION_PIN_LEDGER_MONEYCELLS` does not move. Asserted by
`TestADivergenceVectorGradesNoMoneyCell`.

## 3. THE COUNTING RULE — AND THE ONE CONFLATION, DECLARED

**A DIVERGENCE PASS IS NOT A PARITY PASS.** `DivergencePass` is its own counter and is counted
nowhere in `ParityPass`. `ledger parity PASS 7` still means seven vectors on which this port
reproduced the reference oracle. An open disagreement cannot inflate it.

**A DIVERGENCE FAIL *IS* ADDED TO `ParityFail`.** This is asymmetric on purpose — *a divergence
is never evidence FOR the port and always evidence AGAINST it when it moves* — and it is also
**forced by the grant boundary**, which I am declaring rather than presenting as a free choice:

- `nexus/internal/apps/loanschedule/conformance/grade.go`'s `ExitCode()` computes the run's
  verdict from `s.Ledger.ParityFail + s.Ledger.RefusalFail`;
- `.softhouse/conformance.sh`'s `gate_wrong_ledger_impls_die` reads the same two **printed**
  FAIL figures to decide a wrong port DIED.

Both files are outside my grant. A divergence FAIL that reached neither would be a vector that
cannot turn anything red — decoration. The direction is the safe one: over-reporting a FAIL
raises alarm, never lowers it. The un-conflated form is **PATCH 2/3** of the patch request, and
until then the divergence census prints **both** figures so the fold is visible on the same
page as the number it moved.
