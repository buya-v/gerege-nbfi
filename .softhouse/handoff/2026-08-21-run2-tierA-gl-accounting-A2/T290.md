## 3. F-T290-2 (MAJOR) — "OUT OF DOMAIN" IS A POST-HOC NARROWING, AND IT IS CIRCULAR

The brief warned that *a predicate that fails can always be described as inapplicable*. I tested
the alternative hypothesis and it holds.

**What the predicate's own registration says, quoted rather than inferred** —
`.softhouse/capture/t229-g8-site3/PREDICTION.md:184-186`:

> **P2** — **on every unrescued cell**, `total` of REPAYMENT row 1 = `E`, `total` of the last
> REPAYMENT row = `E + B`, and `totalInterestAmount` = `n·E + B`. **This is the direct observable
> of FACT A**: the last row's excess over the penultimate row IS the disbursement, exactly.

**`T219-R600p0-N103-B1` is unrescued.** `prediction.json` records `site3Rescues: false` and
`predictedOutcome: "LAST_ROW_CARRIES_ALL_PRINCIPAL"`, and `classify_t219.py:139` computes the
`P2_*` keys **only** for non-`RESCUED_BY_SITE3` cells — the classifier's own domain gate passed on
this row. So the predicate is **inside its stated domain and REFUTED**, not outside it.

**The pre-registered silences do not include this case.** `t229/PREDICTION.md` §3 is headed
*"WHERE THIS PREDICATE IS KNOWN TO BE WRONG OR SILENT — written down BEFORE the probe"* and names
exactly three: `E` on rescued cells, the unmodelled rescued branch, and GUARD 3's over-amortization
edge. A cell that amortises early and then emits zero rows is not among them. The authors wrote
their exclusions down in advance precisely so that a later exclusion would be visible as new.

**Did T271 read the domain or infer it?** Inferred. I greped every T271 artefact and its handoff
for `unrescued` — **zero hits** (searched: all 31 files of `f80e1a7` plus the handoff; exit 1, a
true no-match). T271 substitutes its own characterisation: *"P2's first conjunct … is a claim about
the FAMILY-B non-amortising shape."*

**And the substituted domain is circular.** P2's first two conjuncts **are** the assertion of the
EMI-plus-balloon shape. Conditioning P2 on "cells that have that shape" makes P2 **unfalsifiable** —
a predicate cannot take as its precondition the thing it asserts. `P2_emiDifferenceEqualsB` is not
a bystander here: **it is the conjunct that DETECTED the shape's absence.** Calling the detector
inapplicable because it detected something is the reclassification the brief asked me to look for.

**The concrete consequence T271 did not take.** `P2_emiDifferenceEqualsB` is P2's **second**
conjunct, and `t229/PREDICTION.md:192-193` records:

> **The first two conjuncts STAND on every unrescued cell checked, so FACT A is unharmed**

That sentence is hedged by *"checked"* — T241 checked t229 only — but a later reader will take it
as universal, and it is now false of the corpus: **an unrescued cell refutes conjunct two.**
FACT A survives *in substance* only because this cell is not family B and has no balloon at all —
and that qualification belongs **next to the sentence**, not only in a register in another
directory. `P-80`: *a corrected cardinal rots in every place it was restated*, and a corrected
**claim** rots the same way. Labelling the failure "inapplicable" is exactly what removes the
obligation to go back and correct it.

**Mitigating, and it is real.** T271's register `_scopeNote` states the strictly **stronger and
correct** scope point on its own initiative: `t219-g8-residual/PREDICTION.md` registers **no
P1..P6 at all**, so in that directory the `P2_*` keys grade a proposition the probe never
registered. And every disposition pairs "out of domain" with *"the predicate is wrong"*,
*"WITHDRAWN on this row"* and *"T259's EXPECTATION FALSIFIED"*. This is a **wording defect with a
missing follow-through**, not a whitewash — which is why it is a micro-fix and not a rejection.

**The repair** is `PROPOSED-disposition-repair.json`, measured inert (§ verdict). It replaces
`OUT OF ITS DOMAIN` with `REFUTED INSIDE ITS REGISTERED DOMAIN`, quotes the registration and the
three pre-registered silences, states the circularity, keeps T271's mechanism verbatim, and adds
the FACT A consequence.
