cause.

## The graded domain, predicate by predicate — vacuous? prose-vs-list contradictions (the G-5 defect)?

**Yes — R-3, and it is the same defect class as the still-open G-5 on DEC-1.**

**Checkability.** All eleven predicates are mechanically checkable in code without asking the
oracle, as §4.2's preamble claims. `G-01`…`G-06` and `G-09`…`G-11` are set membership or equality
on enumerable domains. No predicate is true-of-nothing. I found **no** unbacked complement: every

manages.

**The vacuity / contradiction.**

- `G-07  Currency.Code == "MNT" and Currency.MinorUnitDigits == 2`
- `G-08  every amount's wire text is EXACT at MinorUnitDigits (no non-zero digit beyond)`

against the prose:

- §2.2 B-5: *"Neither is a parameter and neither appears in the body … **No money flows through
  this seam at all.**"*
- §2.2: *"DEC-2's contract is an **account-selecting function**: its answers are ids, names, GL
  codes, enum values and refusal strings."*
- §3.2: *"…it is a correct statement that this seam is money-free, and **the contract carries no
  amount**."*
- §5: *"because the answers are account ids and strings."*

and against §4.4, which marks **I-1** (debits == credits) and **I-2** (splits sum to whole) **"YES,
gradeable today"**, compared as `int64` minor units.

**I checked whether the data rescues the predicates, and it does — which is what makes the prose
the false half.** `A2-235` holds exactly eight `"amount":` occurrences, every one a bare JSON number
`N.000000`: `1200000` ×2, `200000` ×2, `1000000` ×2, `50000` ×2 [VERIFIED by this task from the raw

`currency_code`: six rows, all `MNT`, all `1200000.000000` [VERIFIED by this task].

So exactly one of these holds, and DEC-2 asserts both:

1. the contract carries no amount → **`G-07` and `G-08` are vacuous** (P-35, true of everything
   because there is nothing to fail them) and §4.4's I-1/I-2 gradings are unreachable; or
2. the contract carries an amount and a currency on `ledger_rest_posting` → **§2.2's and §3.2's

**Not a MICRO-FIX**: the fix decides whether the contract has amount and currency fields, which is
a shape decision *and* a graded-domain-member decision — both explicitly excluded from micro-fix by
my brief.

**One further wrinkle for revision 2.** Even under reading (2), `G-07`/`G-08` are non-vacuous
*only* for `ledger_rest_posting` vectors and vacuous for `ledger_rest_admin` / `ledger_db_readback`

**The one leg that does hold, and it is the strong one.** The capability fatal **is** global:

**And R-1 sits on top of it:** even with the rows authored, no admissible `ledger` vector can be
written to cover them, so the fatal would fire permanently with no legal way to clear it.
