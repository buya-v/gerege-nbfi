## What T177 changed in this section's premise

T177 landed on `main` while T170 was scoped, and it changes what may be *said*, not what was
measured. I have restated it no more strongly than T177 did.

1. **The throw is a function of JVM STATE, not of the cell's inputs.** 139 probe trials, 348 seam
   calls, 75 java processes. Cold start, default `-Xss`, C2 on: the disputed cell (B = 10001 minor,
   n = 3000) threw **33 of 33**; T159's detonation (B = 10001, n = 2000) threw **9 of 9**. Inside a
   JVM the outcome flips to observed at **attempt 5** and never flips back — 7 of 7 JVMs, and no
   `observed` was ever followed by a `threw` in 107 trials of that cell. Generic warm-up on a
   *different* cell buys the same thing (50 calls: 3/3 observed; 1 and 10 calls: 0/3). `-Xss` moves
   it (512k/1m/2m/4m throw 2/2 each; 8m and 16m observe 2/2). With `-XX:TieredStopAtLevel=1` the
   transition **never happens**.
2. **"The throwing region" is not a region of the input space at all.** So G-8's *"not monotone in
   n"* sentence is **refuted at the premise**, not merely imprecise: the two cells differ in run
   position, not behaviour. **T177 did not test the conclusion** ("cannot be excluded by bounding
   the inputs"), and I have written it as untested rather than as supported.
3. **T159 and T169 never disagreed about the oracle.** Replaying T159's committed case list in
   T159's committed order reproduces it cell for cell — the only two cells that throw are the two
   that threw in T159's committed capture, and **24 money comparisons, 0 mismatches**.
4. **The disputed cell is NOT the cell behind G-8's headline residual, and the headline is
   cold-safe.** T177 corrected the driver's own brief on this. The MNT 10.01 residual is
   **B = 1001 minor units**; the throwing cell is **B = 10001**. T177 asked the *headline* cell from
   **9 cold starts: 9 observed, 0 threw**, `totalInterestAmount 15010.01` on all 16 observations.
   **I have written the rebuild so it cannot be read as casting doubt on the headline number — it
   does not, and I say so explicitly in two places.**
5. **`errorStackDepthTotal: 1024` is HotSpot's recording cap, not a depth.** With the cap lifted the
   true depth *rises* with compilation (5119, 4683, 4683, **8400**, then it fits). T177 measured
   depth, not frame size, and asserts no mechanism; nor do I.
