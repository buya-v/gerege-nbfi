## 5. An observed correlation — offered as a DESCRIPTION, not a mechanism

**Family B's cause remains `[UNVERIFIED]`. T117 did not look for it and locates nothing.** What it
can report is a property of the emitted values, in exact arithmetic:

- **155 of 155** family-B cells across both passes have `B·r` — the n → ∞ limit of the annuity
  payment `B·a(r,n)`, in minor units — equal to a **half-integer**. At 600.0 % p.a. the monthly rate

`src/threshold_exact.py` computes the exact gap `B·a − ½` in `fractions.Fraction` and reproduces
T122's committed `+2.4292883e-19` for the n = 104 / B = 1 cell — the rig's calibration against the
record. Nothing above is a claim about which internal quantity rounded where.

## 8. Money discipline, stated explicitly (P-25)

- **The conclusion in §1(ii) is decided in integers.** `residual_census.py` computes
  `unamortized_residual = disbursed_minor − amortized_minor` as an integer subtraction of integer
  minor units. No float is on that path.
- Every money value in these captures is emitted by `BigDecimal.toPlainString()` as a JSON
  **string** and is parsed by splitting on `.` and padding to the currency's decimal places — never
  by `float()`. `json.load` is called with **`parse_float=Decimal`** in every script that reads a
  capture (`classify_t117.py`, `shape_check.py`, `ctrl_reproduction.py`, `tie_profile.py`,
  `rp_reproduction_pass2.py`, `residual_census.py`, `postcheck.py`, `postcheck_pass2.py`), so a
  numeric money literal could not become a binary double even if one appeared.
- Rate arithmetic is `fractions.Fraction` (`threshold_exact.py`, `tie_profile.py`), and the
  half-integer test is the **integer** test `(2·B·r).denominator == 1 and int(2·B·r) % 2 == 1`.
- The only `float()` calls anywhere in this directory are two **display-only** columns in
  `threshold_exact.py`, named `*_display_only_float`, labelled as such in the file's docstring and
  in the emitted JSON, and **read by no comparison**.
- No script that produced committed evidence was edited. `CaptureT117.java` and
  `CaptureT117P2.java` are derived **mechanically** from the committed `CaptureT100.java`, which is
  only read.
