## Method note the coordinator asked about, in one sentence

**Verify-after-`git add`** and **F-1** are the same defect wearing different clothes: F-1 is a
measurement whose *result cannot move when the world changes* (a saturated status), and
verify-before-`git add` is a measurement *taken before the world changed* (an untracked file).
Both are "the check and the thing checked were never in the same state" — which is why running
the bar before `git add` on this branch would have shown a green 109 that was never true of
what I ship, and running it after showed the honest EXIT 2 that named the real reason.
