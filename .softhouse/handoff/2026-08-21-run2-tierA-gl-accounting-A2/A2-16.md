### The decision: **(a) EXTEND** — and DEC-2 grades nothing until the extension exists

**(b) — "grade only what the schema can already express" — is rejected because the intersection is
empty.** No `ledger` input has a field; no `ledger` output has an encoding or a class. (b) is not a
narrower DEC-2, it is a DEC-2 that grades **zero** predicates and admits **zero** vectors, while §0's
whole stated purpose is to give `A2-15` something to build a grader against. A contract that can
never be graded is a memo.

**(a) is adopted, with three normative consequences.**

**First — a SECOND schema and comparator, not a widening of the first.** Alternative recorded and
rejected, with three reasons:
- `StructuralCellFields()`'s safety property is *"exactly the NON-MONEY cells `diffSchedule`
  actually compares"* — a union list covering two comparators is a superset of what either
  compares, which **is** finding T9-F1b, the defect that once printed nine killed capabilities at
  exit 0 over a store of garbage dates.
- **`sentinelByName` returns `contract.Err*` imported from the FROZEN `contract.go`**
  [`enums.go:93-103`, `registry.go:11`]. A fourth sentinel = editing a ratified DEC-1 artefact = a
  hard `user` gate. **The chosen design avoids it entirely**, and §7 now carries a stop-and-raise
  warning for anyone who finds themselves unable to.
- The pipeline is typed on `contract.ScheduleGenerator` / `contract.Schedule`, and **every** vector's
  request is mapped through `Request.ContractRequest()` onto the frozen DEC-1 request
  [`registry.go:26-28`, `:173`, `grade.go:508`]. Widening the schema without widening those types
  produces a vector nothing can be asked to answer.

**Second — the 43 must still pass, and here is how that is demonstrated.** The extension touches the
same harness DEC-1's 43 parity vectors depend on, and they are the only parity evidence this program
has. Written into §5.2 as five requirements:
1. **before/after sha256 of all 43 vector files** — a second schema string means none changes a
   byte; a moved digest proves the first schema was widened, which is out of bounds;
2. **`bash .softhouse/conformance.sh` unfiltered, before and after** — both `VERDICT: PASS (exit 0)`,
   `parity vectors PASS 43 FAIL 0`, and **the same cell count**. Baseline measured on my tree:
   **5664 cells**. A moved cell count means the comparator changed under the loanschedule corpus,
   which is a regression whatever the verdict line says;
3. **`bash .softhouse/conformance.sh loanschedule` before and after**, identical but for timestamps;
4. **no diff to `contract.go` and no DEC-1 amendment** — if either is needed, raise a gate;
5. **`bash`, never `sh`**; never `gofmt -w` `contract.go`.

**Third — Disposition 3 survives on a different argument.** Not *"it needs no new machinery"* (false,
retracted) but *"the machinery is bounded, nameable and separable, and the alternatives are a
deadlock or an unbacked claim"* — **provided** the document says everywhere a reader could be misled
that the grading has not arrived. That proviso is what the banner, §4.4.1, §4.9 and §8.1 are for.

**§5.3 turns this into eight preconditions on `A2-15`**, none of which exists: P-1 request shape,
P-2 oracle-faithful-refusal expectation, P-3 a class for a non-schedule oracle answer, P-4 a

## R-3 decision

**I adopted A2-14's recommendation (ii) — KEEP THE MONEY, NARROW THE PROSE — and I state why, and
where I went further.**

**Why (ii) over (i).** (i) would strike `G-07`/`G-08` and demote `I-1`/`I-2` to "belongs to A1". The
measured data says the prose is the false half, not the predicates:
- `A2-235` — **eight** `"amount":` tokens, each a bare JSON number: `1200000.000000` ×2,
  `200000.000000` ×2, `1000000.000000` ×2, `50000.000000` ×2 [**re-verified by me** from the raw
  bytes]. 120,000,000 + 20,000,000 + 100,000,000 + 5,000,000 = **245,000,000** minor units per side.
- `A2-150` — the `acc_gl_journal_entry` dump projects **both** `amount` and `currency_code`: six
  rows, all `1200000.000000`, all `MNT` [**re-verified by me**, `A2-150-db-final-state.txt:65-70`].

`I-1` and `I-2` are the only two ledger invariants anything in this program could ever grade.
Discarding them to preserve a tidy sentence is the wrong trade.

**Where I went beyond A2-14's recommendation, and why.**

1. **A2-14 suggested scoping `G-07`/`G-08` per seam. I scoped them per ASSERTION instead, because
   per-seam is wrong in both directions** — and I found this by opening the data rather than
   accepting the suggestion. A `ledger_db_readback` vector over `acc_gl_journal_entry` **does** carry
   money (the dump projects it), so "db read-backs are money-free" is false; and a
   `ledger_rest_admin` vector asserting a refusal code carries none. The rule in revision 2 is:
   **`G-07` and `G-08` bind a vector that asserts a money cell, and only such a vector.**
2. **I distinguished *inert by scope* from *vacuous* (P-35) explicitly**, because a reviewer will
   reach for P-35 here. A vacuous predicate is one nothing could fail; these can be failed, by any
   money-carrying vector, and the admission rule must evaluate them on exactly those.
3. **I split §4.4's fourth column in two** — *gradeable in principle from the captures in hand* vs
   *graded today*. `I-1`/`I-2` are **YES / NO**. This is what stops revision 2 from creating a fresh
   contradiction of its own: R-3 makes the money statable, R-1 keeps it ungraded, and a single
   "gradeable?" column could not have said both.
4. **I corrected §5's "most kills will be structural"** — that followed from the over-broad B-5.
   Vectors asserting a journal entry carry real amounts, and `I-1`/`I-2` are **money** kills with
   non-zero margins once P-5 exists.
5. **I corrected §6.6**, which had inherited B-5 to conclude a second currency is a non-event for
   this context. Free for resolution; a value-domain widening for observation, touching a question
   **T186 explicitly left open**.

**The G-5 comparison is drawn explicitly in §2.2**, because the point of resolving R-3 was to avoid
shipping a second ADR with the same open defect, and a future reader should be able to see that the
