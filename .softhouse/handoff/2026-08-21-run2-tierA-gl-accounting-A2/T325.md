## 2. What was added to the guard, and why each addition exists

`.softhouse/guards/repo-state-attest.sh` (T318's file; T325 extends it, changes no term's
meaning):

| addition | why |
|---|---|
| `--writ-ref <ere>` | so `refs/remotes/origin/*` — moved by STEP 5.5's own push — can be authorized. One `ref_in_writ` now answers for T1 and T2; before, two hand-rolled loops over `writ_branches` disagreed with each other by omission. |
| `--allow-deleted-ref <ere>` | the prune sweep's `git branch -d`. Authorization is **name AND measured reachability**, never the name alone. |
| `--artefact-advisory` | a fire merges whatever worker branches went green and cannot enumerate artefacts in advance. A blocking T7 would fire on ordinary merge fires. **Scoped concession**: mutation row M3 shows T7 goes back to blocking the moment the flag is absent. |
| `survey <repo>` | the single-state subset, for the two gates that have **no** "before" — pre-flight (nothing of this run has happened yet) and the worktree sweep (the worktrees that matter were created *during* the fire). Votes on exactly one term. |
| `fire-compare <b> <a>` | the fire's writ, defined **once**. The wrapper calls it and the drives call it, so a green in the drive is a green about the writ that actually runs (P-45's shape one level up). |

**The boundary T318 argued is intact.** Ancestry, not equality: every legitimate commit is a
fast-forward, and the deletion rule is the same idea applied to disappearance (*is the tip
still reachable?*). **Reflog, `git fsck` dangling objects and object counts remain excluded
with T318's reasons, and I do not argue against them** — see the R1 arm below, which is the
one place their absence is visible, driven and declared rather than hidden.

**The oracle-vs-self asymmetry is preserved, deliberately.** The 45 gates over
`/Users/buv/fineract` are correct *because* they are PINNED to
`EXPECTED_FINERACT_COMMIT` — the reference oracle is meant to be immobile. This repo is
meant to move, which is why it gets a DIFFERENTIAL check and not a pinned one. Nothing in
T325 touches the oracle checkout, and the wiring census prints that exclusion as part of its
scope statement rather than leaving it implied.
