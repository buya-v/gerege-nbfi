### The sixth mechanism — the addition to the STANDING RULE, and why it is not decoration

The rule's preamble catalogued five ways a wrong number reached Buyan. The fifth's prescribed remedy
is: *"when a sentence's domain is the set of cells somebody happened to run, the fix is a predicate,
not a wider sweep."*

**T223 followed that remedy exactly — derived the region from the mechanism, registered it in an
ancestor commit, probed — and the result was still wrong by a factor of three.** So the fifth
mechanism's remedy is not sufficient, and recording the ceiling correction without recording *that*
would leave the next worker with a method the record has already defeated.

The sixth is written as: **a derived predicate inherits every unmeasured quantity it is built on, and
states the result with the confidence of a derivation.** The rescue condition has two terms; T223
wrote one and silently set the other (`δ`) to zero because `E` had never been measured. The new
clause: **when you replace a sweep with a predicate, enumerate the predicate's inputs and say which
of them you measured** — anything unmeasured is a conjecture the conclusion rests on and belongs
*next to the conclusion*, not in a footnote. It also records that **refusing to edit this section is a
legitimate outcome of the rule** (T117, T159, T229) and must not be read as an omission.
