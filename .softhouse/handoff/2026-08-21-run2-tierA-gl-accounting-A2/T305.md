## Money-math notes

**No arithmetic was performed on any money value in this task, and none was recorded.** No
vector was captured, no expected value was authored, no amount was converted, summed,
rounded or compared. The rig contains **no monetary literal of any kind** — its queries count
rows, read sequence values, read configuration integers and read time-zone strings. There is
consequently no float anywhere, including in intermediate calculation, and nothing to check
against the minor-unit rule.

The one money-adjacent finding is a **configuration** one, and it decides the whole task:

- `gerege` `c_configuration.rounding-mode` = **4**; `default` = **6**.
- `java.math.RoundingMode` ordinals: `HALF_UP` = 4, `HALF_EVEN` = 6. CLAUDE.md ratifies
  `HALF_UP` for MNT and names **4** explicitly.
- So `default` runs at **HALF_EVEN**, at `Asia/Kolkata` (+05:30), and `.softhouse/reference-oracle.md`
  already records it as the deliberate **wrong-offset / HALF_EVEN negative control**.
- **CLAUDE.md's own consequence rule** — that captures taken at a non-ratified arithmetic
  setting are *"discrimination probes, not parity vectors"* — therefore disqualifies any
  capture taken there **by the project's rule, not by my preference**. That is C1/C2 of the
  gate, and it is why the one tenant where `:812` would fall through is the one whose result
  could not be called a parity vector.

The **irreversibility** argument is likewise structural, not arithmetic:
`saveAllDebitOrCreditOpeningBalanceEntries` persists **two** entries per leg (`:791` the leg,
`:796` its contra); `JournalEntriesApiResource` exposes only `@GET`/`@POST` and there is **no
`@DELETE` anywhere under `fineract-provider/.../accounting/`**; `deleteGLAccount` (`:191-212`)
refuses with `TRANSACTIONS_LOGGED` once any entry references an account. So the setup becomes
undeletable **by the capture's own success**, and the posted entries are exactly what makes
`findNonContraTransactionIds` non-empty — **the capture destroys its own precondition**.
That is P-92 (*"the danger is highest immediately after the capture SUCCEEDS — because taking
the observation is often what removes the precondition"*) in its purest form, and unlike a
refusal probe **there is no content fence available**: a body that cannot post is not an
accept. Hence a gate on the **tenant**, not on the body.
