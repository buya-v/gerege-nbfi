### What the defect actually IS — the quantity is right, the LABEL is wrong

**`n·E + B` is the TOTAL REPAYMENT, not the total interest.** On both cells it equals the observed
`totalRepaymentAmount` **exactly** (4503001, 6751499), and the overstatement of interest equals the
principal repaid **exactly** (1, 1499).

**And the corrected form is a consequence of `site3.py`'s OWN derivation, needing no new mechanism
and no new measurement.** S3.1/S3.5 establish that rows 1..n−1 each total `E` and the last totals
`E + B`, so the total column sums to `(n−1)·E + (E + B) = n·E + B`. On the G-8 shape there are no
fees and no penalties, so each row's `total` is `interest + principal` — my script **asserts that
per row**, on all 3,000 rows of each cell — hence interest = `n·E + B − principal`. The error was a
missing subtraction, and it is invisible whenever the principal repaid is 0.
