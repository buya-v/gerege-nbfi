  [VERIFIED: `analysis/shipped_literals.json`, key
  `checkPeriod_emi_dueInterest_duePrincipal_outstandingBalance`].

literals into binary doubles at read time**, and `:159`'s `want = [str(x) for x in row]` then renders
`0.00` as **`'0.0'`**. **The scale the file's own header says was "copied character for character"
is destroyed before any comparison happens.**
