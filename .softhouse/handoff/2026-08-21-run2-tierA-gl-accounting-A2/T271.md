## 2. WHY it fails, and why T259 did not flag it

### The predicate is out of its domain. The data is right, the verdict is right, the *claim* is inapplicable.

Measured row by row from the raw gz (`out/independent-recheck-output.txt`):

```
  rows with nonzero INTEREST  : [1 … 13]        (13 rows × 1 minor unit = 13)
  rows with nonzero PRINCIPAL : [14]            (1 minor unit, on row 14 of 103)
  rows with nonzero TOTAL     : [1 … 14]        ROWS 15–103 ARE ALL ZERO
  row-1 total E 1, last row total 0, emiDifference -1 vs B 1
  n*E+B = 104;  n*E+B-P = 103;  MEASURED total repayment = 14;  interest = 13
  only 13 of 103 rows ever charge interest, so n*E prices 90 rows that charge nothing.
```

Both `P2_*` conjuncts describe the **family-B EMI-plus-balloon shape**: `n` instalments each
totalling `E`, with the balloon `B` riding on the last row. Under that shape
`totalRepayment = n·E + B` and — since `interest = totalRepayment − principalRepaid` is a
definitional identity — `interest = n·E + B − P` follows. **This cell has no such shape.** It is
the one cell in the set registered as *clean — it amortises*, and it does: it repays at row 14 and
then emits 89 zero rows. The `E_modal` column makes it visible without any modelling — every other
carrier has `E_modal == E_row1`, this one has `E_modal = 0` against `E_row1 = 1`.

So, to answer the four possibilities the brief named:

* **The data is not wrong.** My raw sums equal the capture's own header totals on all 7 carriers,
  and equal every `observed*` field `classify_t219.py` recorded, 7 of 7.
* **The verdict is not wrong.** `verdict` grades exactly two things — observed outcome *family*
  against `predictedOutcome`, and `observedPrincipalMinor == predictedTotalPrincipalMinor`. Both
  held here (registered *amortises*, observed `AMORTIZES_FULLY(last-row-only)`; predicted
  principal 1 == observed 1).
* **The predicate is wrong — in two different ways on two different sets of rows.** On `-B4499`
  and `-B3001` it is *misstated* (`n·E + B` is the total **repayment** column, not the interest
  column) and T259's corrected form repairs it exactly. On `-B1` it is *out of domain*: there is no
  corrected form of "`lastRowTotal − row1Total == B`" for a schedule with no balloon row, and the
  corrected interest form is conditional on a structure this cell does not have.
* **The expectation is wrong.** T259 wrote *"the expected outcome is the same (the registered
  predicate is wrong, the verdicts stand)"* and singled out `-B4499` as the row to re-check
  "because the principal repaid is 1,499 and the arithmetic is worth re-checking". **`-B4499`
  behaves exactly as expected. `-B1`, which T259 did not name, does not.**

### Why T259 missed it — and it is not carelessness, it is the shape of the instrument

T259's blind spot was **arithmetic magnitude, not structure**. It reasoned about which row's
arithmetic was *risky* and picked the one with a large principal repaid (1,499) — a bigger
correction term means a bigger chance of getting the correction wrong. `-B1` has principal repaid
of **1 minor unit**, the smallest possible non-zero value, so on that reasoning it was the *safest*
row in the set. The correction term is not what breaks it; the **row structure** is, and structure
is invisible to a check that only asks whether `n·E + B − P` reconciles. **A number that is wrong
in the direction of the instrument's own blind spot** — precisely as the brief framed it — and the
blind spot is real, not coincidental.

The second half is P-79 in its own right: T259 measured B-1 correctly, wrote the row ids down
correctly, and then **recorded an assumption about them in the same paragraph that said they must
be re-derived rather than assumed.** *Evidence is not missing, it is UNREAD* — here, unread by its
own author, one sentence later.

### Systematic or isolated? **Both, and they are different defects.**

* **SYSTEMATIC — the verdict/predicate disagreement itself.** It is structural, not incidental:
  `verdict` is assigned at `classify_t219.py:132`/`137` and every `P2_*` key at `140-142`,
  **strictly after**. The field *cannot* read them, in any capture, on any row. The same classifier
  is inherited verbatim by `t229-g8-site3` and `t223-g8-region-predicate`. It will recur in any
  future probe that inherits it, and R-VPA is the correct systemic answer — this task is its second
  application, not a one-off.
* **SYSTEMATIC — the `n·E + B` misstatement.** Same registered predicate, same error, two captures,
  five rows across them (t229: 3; t219: 2). T259's corrected form is right and repairs all five.
* **ISOLATED — the corrected form failing.** Exactly **one** row in **one** capture, and it is
  isolated *for a stated reason*: it is the only carrier in either capture whose schedule is not
  EMI-plus-balloon (`structureHolds = 6 of 7`, and the sole exception is that row). This is not
  "one bad row we could not explain"; the explanation is measured and the discriminator is printed.
