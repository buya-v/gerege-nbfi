### F-A — the micro-fix. `ApplicableSlotName` now carries the caller's family

**The shape A2-9 suggested was checked against the actual code before being used, and it is
right, with one simplification A2-9 did not propose.**

- `resolve.go:283` now passes the typed `slot`, not `code`.
- `mappingNotFound`'s signature is `(keyType PortfolioProductType, productID int64, slot Slot)`;
  it takes `code := slot.Code()` and sets `applicable := slot.String()`.

  if/else collapses to the single `fallbackName(...)` call in both branches. The byte proof
  below is what establishes this rather than the argument.
- `mappingNotFound` has exactly **one** call site
  [VERIFIED: `LC_ALL=C /usr/bin/grep -n 'mappingNotFound' nexus/internal/apps/ledger/*.go`
  → `resolve.go:283` (call), `:292` (doc), `:322` (declaration); nothing else].

  `ResolveWorkingCapitalLoanProductAccount` both refuse a slot whose `ProductFamily()` is not
