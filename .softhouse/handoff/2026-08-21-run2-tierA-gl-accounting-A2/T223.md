### 3. The predicate, registered BEFORE the probe

`.softhouse/capture/t223-g8-region-predicate/PREDICTION.md` + `prediction.json`, committed at
**`c2771fb`** — a **strict ancestor** of `fdddd1c`, which carries the capture. `[VERIFIED — git log]`

In integer minor units, with `I₁ = B_minor · r` exact (a `Fraction`, never rounded) and `E_q` the
oracle's instalment re-executed digit-for-digit and quantized HALF_UP to dp 2:

| | |
|---|---|
| `E_q > I₁` | not family B |
| `E_q = I₁` | not family B (residual lands as principal) |
| `E_q < I₁` | **FAMILY B**, unless site 3 rescues it (`B_minor > ⌊n/2⌋` **and** `2·B_minor ≥ n`) |

**In the phenomenon's own variables:** the excess `E_exact − I₁ = I₁·(1+r)^−n/(1−(1+r)^−n)` decays
geometrically, so the **term** enters only as `(1+r)^n` vs `10^19` — boundary term
`n* ≈ 19 / log₁₀(1+r)`, **a property of the RATE alone** — and the **principal** enters only as
*resonance* (`I₁` on a half-minor-unit boundary) and as site 3's *rescue ceiling* `B_minor ≳ n/2`.

**This explains, rather than restates, the "all 20 family-B principals are odd" observation**: at
600 % `r = 1/2`, so `B_minor · r` is a half-integer **iff `B_minor` is odd**. At 36 % the same
condition reads `B_minor ≡ 50 (mod 100)`; at 300 %, `B_minor ≡ 2 (mod 4)`.
