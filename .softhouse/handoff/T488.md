### Three findings that changed the plan's shape

1. **Fineract's own tests never post a manual journal entry for its own sake, and never reverse one.**
   `grep -rn journalentries` over the whole integration corpus finds exactly **one** direct
   `POST /journalentries` call site, used by **one** test, and only to make a `DELETE` fail. There is
   **no `glclosures` test anywhere in the tree** (zero hits). This program's own ledger corpus is
   already ahead of Fineract's on the manual-posting surface.

2. **Every read-back URL in `JournalEntryHelper` hard-codes `tenantIdentifier=default`**
   (`:140`, `:163`, `:185`). Tenant `default` on our instance is `Asia/Kolkata` +05:30, `HALF_EVEN`,
   with **0 GL accounts and 0 journal entries**. A recipe copied verbatim out of Fineract's tests
   reads the wrong tenant and finds nothing, silently. Every recipe in the plan sends the tenant
   header explicitly.

3. **The savings/deposit half of the accounting test corpus is unusable, on LEGAL grounds.**
   `AccountingScenarioIntegrationTest` spends 5 of 11 tests on savings/fixed/recurring deposit
   accounting. NBFI (ББСБ): Law on Non-Banking Financial Activities Art. 12.1.3 / 12.1.4, and
   reference-oracle POLICY item 2 — *"No deposit or savings behaviour"*. ~7,500 LOC / 97 tests
   rejected on that ground. No case in the plan touches a deposit endpoint.

## 4. Everything runs on a THROWAWAY, and that is a policy conclusion, not a convenience

Not one case is routed at the standing `gerege` oracle. Reference-oracle § *POLICY* ranks a throwaway
above the standing instance for any accepted write that cannot be un-accepted, and the plan's cases
are exactly that shape: they need **new products with charge-off mappings**, **a second office**,
**a closure**, **non-MNT currencies** and **GL-account deletes** — every one of which the POLICY either
forbids outright on `gerege` (*"Never a … closure"*, *"Never a … GL-account edit"*) or makes permanent.

**Consequence for `PROBES.tsv`: nothing to add.** No case in this plan touches the standing oracle, so
`oracle-state-baseline.sh` is unaffected. **If a later fire moves any case to STANDING, that obligation
attaches immediately** and the plan says so at §4.12(5).

A complete throwaway rig template already exists — `.softhouse/capture/t327-closure-accepting-side/throwaway/` —
seeded at `Asia/Ulaanbaatar` and `FINERACT_CONFIG_ROUNDING_MODE: "4"`, with `guard-throwaway-isolation.sh`
refusing unless it provably cannot touch the standing stack. The plan says **copy it, change five
identifiers, do not build a second one**.

would have been the exact error CLAUDE.md names when it says the `D-*` captures are discrimination
probes, not parity vectors.

## 6. One instrument correction the next fire needs

**`cap8.sh` must not be used for a POST.** It sends **no `Idempotency-Key`**, so Fineract mints a random
UUID (`IdempotencyKeyResolver.java:36`, `IdempotencyKeyGenerator.java:25-29`) and the write is
**unattributable forever** — the failure mode T371 documented. Use **`cap11.sh`**
(`.softhouse/capture/t352-a2-next-tranche/`), which **refuses** when the key is absent. `cap8.sh` remains
correct for `GET`. Key convention for this plan: `T488-<CASE>-<ARM>`, one distinct key per request.

## 8. What I got wrong, found myself, and corrected

**The first draft asserted that `GLAccount` carries an `is_deleted` column. It does not.** The claim was
inferred from a neighbouring fact about `GLClosure` in `.softhouse/reference-oracle.md` and never checked.
A pre-commit sweep of the document's own citations caught it. Measured instead: `acc_gl_account` is created
once at `0001_initial_schema.xml:49-75` with **no deleted marker**, the whole changelog tree references that
table on exactly **three** lines (all in that file), and `GLAccount.java` carries no `@SQLDelete` and no
deleted field. **Corrected in place with the error named** (§8 item 4 and §10 of the plan), and downgraded
from a fact to a `SOURCE-DERIVED HYPOTHESIS` that `TDG-A4`'s SQL arm measures.

A second draft claim — *"needs a JDK this sandbox does not have"* — was also false (`openjdk 21.0.10` is
present). Corrected: the obstacle is scope and the read-only pinned checkout, not tooling.

**No other claim was found unsupported by that sweep.**
